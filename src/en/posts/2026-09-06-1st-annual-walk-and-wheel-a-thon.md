# First Annual Walk and Wheel-a-thon

Let's walk, roll, and make a BIG difference together!

__Thursday Sept 25th, 1:30PM-3:30PM__

## What is the Walk & Wheel-a-thon?

On September 25th, students will walk or roll laps with their class to raise money for G.W. Childs!

Funds raised will support things such as school events, classroom materials, enrichment opportunities, and field trips.

## How to Donate

Donate directly to the classroom of your choice!
The classroom with the most money raised & the classroom that shows the most school spirit will win special prizes & a pizza lunch sponsored by the Home & School Association (HSA).

Donate on Zeffy here: [https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/ticketing/gw-childs-walkathon](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/ticketing/gw-childs-walkathon)

### Business Sponsors Welcome!

Do you own a business? Sponsor the Walk & Wheel-a-thon and your business will be featured in our newsletters, social media, and HSA communications.

THANK YOU FOR SUPPORTING GW CHILDS!
