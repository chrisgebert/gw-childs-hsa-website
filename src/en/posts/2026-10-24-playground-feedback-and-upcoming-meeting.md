# Playground Feedback and Upcoming Meeting

Thank you to everyone who was able to attend the first School Yard feedback session! It was helpful to get a breakdown of what's being considered with each of the three playground designs and how we can give the students a renovated recess area.

If you missed or want to review the presentation from Studio Ludo it's [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gBMT0g6dTMflidrN_Gz0j9q26LJbT27h/view?usp=sharing&utm_source=gwchildshsa&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=gw-childs-playground-feedback-upcoming-meeting) and includes examples of each design, their thought process, and student workshops discussing what they'd like to see in the space.

Please add your feedback/thoughts directly to the presentation above using the final slide or by directly commenting on a concept (preferred) _OR_ email either [jen@jendevor.com](mailto:jen@jendevor.com) or [GWChildsHSA@gmail.com](mailto:GWChildsHSA@gmail.com) with the subject **G.W.** **Childs Playground Comments**.

The next feedback session is less than two weeks away on Thursday, Nov 4th during the second half of the HSA meeting in the Library. We hope to see you there and hear more of your thoughts!
