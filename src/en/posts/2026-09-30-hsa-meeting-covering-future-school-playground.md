# HSA Meeting Covering Future School Playground

**Schoolyard Reimagining Project Updates: ** The HSA sub-committee will be hosting [Studio Ludo](https://www.studioludo.org/?utm_source=gwchildshsa&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=hsa-meeting-covering-future-school-playground) - the rooftop K-4 play area designers - to begin collecting feedback on the ground level area to redesign the current lot for the older grades. 

Please join us at this week's HSA meeting on Oct 1st in the Library at 4:30 or virtually on [Zoom here](https://temple.zoom.us/meeting/register/hx_stgHqTFWzoY3NSQC6fA?utm_source=gwchildshsa&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=hsa-meeting-covering-future-school-playground). 

This will be an _extended _meeting with the first half handling monthly upkeep while the second half will focus on opening a dialogue with caregivers to gather feedback on the schoolyard's future. During that, Studio Ludo's committee will introduce the framework of what's ahead in the redesign process to develop an enriching environment for the students.

We would like to hear from everyone in the community to work towards building an area for all our students to find growth in play, interaction, and enjoyment so having your individual feedback is helpful. 

RSVP here and if you can't attend, please email your thoughts to [gwchildshsa@gmail.com](mailto:gwchildshsa@gmail.com) with the subject line: **G.W. Childs Playground Redesign**.

Refreshments will be served.
