# Meeting Notes for HSA Meeting, September 2026

Welcome back to a new school year! The G.W. Childs HSA kicked off the year and shared several exciting updates:

- **Programs & Support:** Learn about before/after care, break camps, the _Mindset of Champions_ tech & robotics club, and the community Family Market providing fresh produce.
- **Playground Project:** Thanks to a $25,000 grant, design concepts for a refreshed playground are underway! Studio Ludo will present design concepts at our next HSA meeting.
- **Get Involved:** We need parent volunteers to support our school library, Family Market, and schoolyard cleanups. Free English classes will also be offered in the library every Thursday!
- **Upcoming Fundraisers:** Get ready for our **Walk & Wheel-a-thon**, an inclusive event celebrating all students while raising funds for our school! Plus, check out our new school swag online and look out for election day bake sales.

📅 **Join Us:** HSA meetings take place on the **1st Thursday of every month** in person and on Zoom. Stay connected via our newsletter and WhatsApp group!
