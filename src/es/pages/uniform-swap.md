## Uniform Swap

<span class="image right">![](/assets/images/uniform-swap.jpg)</span>

Our school’s Uniform Swap is a community-driven initiative designed to help families save money while promoting sustainability. Parents and caregivers are encouraged to donate gently used, outgrown uniform pieces—such as shirts, pants, and sweatshirts——that are still in good condition——for other students to use.

The swap operates on a "give what you can, take what you need" basis, ensuring every student has access to the required school attire regardless of their budget. It’s a fantastic way to declutter your closets, reduce textile waste, and support our fellow school families all at once.

### How to Participate

- Donate: Drop off cleaned, stain-free uniforms at the HSA office during school hours.

- Shop: Stop by the HSA office to browse the available sizes during our weekly HSA office hours.

- Volunteer: Help us sort donations by size and type to keep the exchange organized.
